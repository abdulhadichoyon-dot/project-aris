What is Aris?

A digital character built around conversation first. The interface is only the stage. The real product is the feeling that someone is there, listening.

THE CORE IDEA

Not another chat box.
A character you can talk to.

Aris is designed around a simple loop: you speak, the system understands, the AI responds, and the avatar reacts. Text and voice become one continuous experience rather than separate features.
